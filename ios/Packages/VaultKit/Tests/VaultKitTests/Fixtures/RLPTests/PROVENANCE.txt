The two JSON files in this directory are copied verbatim from the
ethereum/tests suite, which is the published reference for what RLP may and may
not encode. They are here because this project's RLP decoder claims strictness,
and a claim of strictness needs the reference's own counter-examples: all 26
vectors in invalidRLPTest.json must be refused.
