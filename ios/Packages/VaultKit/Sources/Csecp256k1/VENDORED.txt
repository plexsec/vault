RECOVERY_MODULE=src/modules/recovery/main_impl.h is byte-identical to upstream
v0.8.0 (sha256 fbec2358a560b669d85720108b0b1a8f653c91531a88ded0a1da339445266cc9,
6135 bytes). It is the only vendored file added after the initial import, it is
unmodified, and it is compiled by defining ENABLE_MODULE_RECOVERY for this target.
It is needed because the v0.2 signing flow must recover a public key from a
signature to determine the signature's recovery value and to verify the card's
output; that is not derivable from point arithmetic alone. The module's signing
entry point (secp256k1_ecdsa_sign_recoverable) is not reachable from Swift: the
wrapper in Sources/VaultKit/Crypto/Secp256k1.swift exposes recover and verify
only, and tools/audit-release.sh asserts no signing entry point is referenced.
